# ECAT/ICAT Falsification and Limits

## Bounded falsifiers

The repository's current formal claims are falsified or invalidated if any of the following occur:

1. a profile that omits a schema-required ECAT or ICAT field is accepted as valid by the canonical validator;
2. the committed RC1 fixture outcomes diverge from the committed expected report without an admitted contract change;
3. ECAT or ICAT output is treated as execution authority rather than pre-boundary standing evidence;
4. relationship intuition is accepted as proof without declared entities/context required by the ICAT schema;
5. missing recoverability evidence is silently promoted to an authoritative ALLOW;
6. replayed or reconstructed historical standing is treated as present authority without later governance evaluation;
7. downstream round-trip examples bypass BCAT/GCAT rather than carry bounded ECAT/ICAT context into them.

## Limits

The repository does not establish:

- psychological diagnosis;
- emotional surveillance legitimacy;
- universal measures of coherence, trust, consent, or relationship quality;
- calibrated population-level probabilities;
- execution authority;
- publication authority;
- final AE admissibility;
- permanent authority from a historically valid profile.

## Failure posture

When evidence required by the repository's schemas or declared contract is absent, malformed, stale, or not reconstructable, downstream consumers must not infer stronger standing than the source profile supports.

A repository validation PASS proves only that the bounded committed artifacts satisfy the encoded checks. It does not prove universal correctness or authorize a real-world transition.

## Change discipline

Changing a schema, fixture contract, expected result, or downstream meaning requires the corresponding tests, receipt evidence, handoff state, and consumer assumptions to be reconciled. Validators must not be weakened merely to preserve a desired PASS result.
